# Project Overview

## Positioning

**Industrial Project Cost, Productivity & Profitability Analytics** is an industrial operations analytics case study inspired by the business problems and responsibilities described in an Engineering Internship role at TKIL Industries.

- All data is **synthetic** and generated by code in this repository.
- The company analysed, **ProjectCo**, is **fictional**.
- No TKIL data, projects, clients or systems are used, represented or implied.

## The company (fictional)

ProjectCo is a mid-sized Indian heavy-engineering and EPC company. It designs, procures, fabricates, installs and commissions plant equipment for process industries. It runs three fabrication works and executed 80 projects (₹7,021 Cr) over FY22-FY26, with contract values from ₹15 Cr to ₹450 Cr. Most contracts are fixed-price, so cost and schedule overruns reduce ProjectCo's own profit. Full parameters are in [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md).

## The business question

> **Which projects are at financial or operational risk, why are they at risk, and what actions can management take to improve cost, productivity, schedule and profitability?**

| Part | Analysis type | Delivered as |
|---|---|---|
| Which projects? | Descriptive | KPIs, thresholds, risk score |
| Why? | Diagnostic | Cost-driver decomposition, Pareto, root-cause tracing across procurement, operations and schedule |
| What to do? | Prescriptive | As-Is/To-Be process redesign, business cases, ROI and payback, prioritised recommendations |

## Question themes

- **Financial:** budget overruns, cost drivers, estimated vs actual profit and margin, margin deterioration.
- **Operational:** labour productivity, machine utilisation, downtime, output achievement, defects, bottlenecks.
- **Procurement:** supplier reliability, lead time, price, quality, and whether cheaper suppliers really lower total project cost.
- **Project management:** delays, the lifecycle stages that cause them, and what delay costs in rupees.
- **Management:** where to cut cost, which improvement to fund first, which projects need attention now.
